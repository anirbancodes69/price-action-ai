## 0. Summary

1. **The 14 "unverifiable" price-scale sessions are resolved (VERIFIED).** A stronger test compares the 5m session open, high and low with NSE's day open, high and low. On the 23,495 traded-scale sessions every session matches at least one value exactly (22,660 match all three). On the 8,991 provider-adjusted sessions **none** match. The corrected matrix is **23,509 traded-scale / 8,991 provider-adjusted / 0 unverifiable**, and **tick status 23,509 pass / 8,991 unverifiable / 0 fail** (§3).
2. **Two repository facts change the plan (VERIFIED by reading the code).** (a) The research harness already has a constant list of special sessions (`SPECIAL_SESSIONS`) that **keeps** the three full-length weekend sessions; my Stage A eligibility excluded all seven weekend/Muhurat dates. The two differ by 150 instrument-sessions (N-1). (b) The backtest loader reads each instrument's tick from `instrument_snapshots` and falls back to 0.05; for the 29 mixed instruments that is the demo placeholder and for the 21 others there is no tick before 2026-11-19, so historical sessions use 0.05 even when the real tick was 0.01, 0.10, 0.50 or 1.00 (N-2). The M1 screen also hard-codes `TICK = 0.05` (N-3).
3. **The 4b trade-floor worry is smaller than it looked, but only a measurement settles it.** Planning figures give far more candidate signals per session than the two position slots can use, so the binding limits in 4b are slot occupancy and the daily loss rules, not signal supply. The floor of 100 over 60 sessions needs about 1.67 qualifying filled trades per session (0.83 at 120 sessions). That is plausible but thin at 60 sessions. Stage A as written runs experiment 4a only (plan §4.1), so it does **not** produce 4b throughput; the owner has three options to learn it before the Stage C freeze (§4.2).
4. **The critical path is governance plus engineering, not data.** The data track is largely prepared; the longest items are E6 with its calibration, the human review (DR-7a), and the decision batch itself (§5).
5. **Nothing may be built until OD-8 is approved.** §6 gives specifications and waits.

### 4.2 The 4b floor of 100 trades (arithmetic from existing parameters; no price or return data used)

**Constraints, from the released ruleset and the planning values.** Capital ₹1,00,000; risk per trade 0.5% = ₹500; maximum open risk 1.0% = ₹1,000, so **2 open positions** (`default_max_open_positions` 2); maximum position value ₹50,000; daily loss limit 1.5% = ₹1,500; at most 3 consecutive losses; stop distance 0.15-2.0%; entry cutoff 45 minutes before close.

**Derived.** Position value = ₹500 / stop fraction, so the ₹50,000 cap binds whenever the stop is **below 1.0%** of the entry; those trades risk less than ₹500. A stock above ₹50,000 could not be traded; none of the 50 exceeds that.

**Required rate.** 100 filled trades over 60 sessions needs **1.67 per session**; over 120 sessions **0.83 per session**. Over 60 sessions, a rate λ per session gives: 0.5 → 30; 1.0 → 60; 1.5 → 90; 1.67 → 100; 2.0 → 120; 2.5 → 150; 3.0 → 180 (and double at 120 sessions).

**What is known and unknown.** Candidate supply: the plan's planning scale is about 0.4 trades per instrument-session, about 20 per session over 50 instruments (a planning figure from the M1 screen, **not a result**). Supply far exceeds the two slots, so λ is set by how long positions stay open, the daily loss and consecutive-loss stops, and how many signals pass the risk gates. Those quantities are **unknown without the counts P0-1b would give**. With two slots and positions that close within the session, a rate of about 2 to 3 per session is plausible (INFERRED), which clears the floor at 60 sessions with a margin of about 20-80% and clears it easily at 120.

**4a.** Eligible inputs are 32,145 instrument-sessions (32,295 with N-1). The 1,000-trade gate needs 3.1% of them to produce a filled B0 trade; the planning scale is about 13 times that. The prospective precision rule (half-width ≤ 0.05R at 99%) is the binding 4a requirement, not the floor; M1 needed about 1,000 trades for that width, and 60 sessions × 50 instruments × 0.4 is about 1,200 (planning), so the margin is thin and the 120-session look is likely needed.

**What P0-1b would add.** Counts, by month and instrument, of B0 signals, fills passing `RC-04`, and (with the 4b rules) filled trades per session; the distribution of concurrent positions and the number of days on which the daily loss rule stops trading; no returns, no edge. Stage A as written runs 4a only, so 4b throughput is learned only by (1) a counts-only P0-1b limited to 4b, (2) a counts-only 4b replay of B0 added to the pre-registered Stage A diagnostics (a plan amendment; my recommendation), or (3) the prospective data themselves (the risk: `INCONCLUSIVE_TRADE_COUNT` after months of capture).
